No one should feel alone. We exist to create relationship and connection because everyone deserves to belong.

Safe Families is a charity that works with 35+ local authorities around the UK. Safe Families offer hope, belonging and support to children, families and care leavers; we do this primarily, but not exclusively, with and through local churches.

Safe Families works with Local Authorities, Churches and Community Groups across the UK.

Safe Families model is flexible, trauma informed and takes a whole family approach to change. We train and support volunteers who can provide emotional and practical support, overnight hosting and resources support. Volunteers often form long term relationships with families, improving their social networks.
