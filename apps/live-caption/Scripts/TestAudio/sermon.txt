Good morning church, and welcome to Destiny Church. It is so good to have you with us today, whether you are here in the room or watching online.
If you have your Bible with you, turn with me to the book of Philippians, chapter four. We are going to read from verse six.
Do not be anxious about anything, but in every situation, by prayer and petition, with thanksgiving, present your requests to God.
[[slnc 1500]]
Now some of us came in this morning carrying a lot. Maybe it was a hard week at work. Maybe it was a phone call you were not expecting, or a bill that arrived at the worst possible time.
Paul writes these words from a prison cell. He is not sitting on a beach. He is chained to a guard, and he still tells this church to bring everything to God with thanksgiving.
[[slnc 2500]]
Let me show you three things from this passage. First, worry is not a weakness to be ashamed of, it is an invitation to pray. Second, thanksgiving changes the way we see our problems. And third, the peace of God is promised, but it is not explained.
Look at verse seven. And the peace of God, which transcends all understanding, will guard your hearts and your minds in Christ Jesus.
The word guard there is a military word. It describes soldiers standing at the gate of a city through the night, so that the people inside can sleep.
[[slnc 6000]]
In the book of Isaiah, chapter twenty six, the prophet says that God will keep in perfect peace those whose minds are steadfast, because they trust in him.
And Jesus says in the Gospel of John, peace I leave with you, my peace I give you. I do not give to you as the world gives.
So here is my challenge for this week. Every time you feel that knot in your stomach, stop and name one thing you are thankful for, and then hand the worry over in prayer.

In a moment the worship team is going to come back up, and we are going to respond together. If you would like someone to pray with you, our prayer team will be at the front, and they would love to meet you.
Let us pray. Father, thank you that you hear us. Thank you that we can bring you every burden we are carrying today. Guard our hearts and our minds, and fill this place with your peace. In Jesus name, Amen.
