Lighting cues

For each buzzer: the Titan playback number, what to do with it, and how many times in how many seconds. Leave the number empty to fire nothing. Go fires the playback; Flash and Swop press those buttons (Flash needs flash turned on for that playback in Titan); Fader moves it to the level you set. Done once, Go, Swop and Fader stay on until Reset; done several times they pulse on and off. Test runs it without touching the screen.

Sounds

Pick a sound file (MP3, WAV or M4A) from this computer to replace a sound. The screen uses it straight away. Built-in goes back to the original sound.
