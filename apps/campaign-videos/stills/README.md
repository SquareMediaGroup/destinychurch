# Stills

Every screen of the `DownloadToday` film as a 1920x1080 JPEG, taken once each scene has settled.
Files ending `-typing`, `-question`, `-answer` and `-zoom` are extra moments inside a scene.
Remake them all with `node scripts/export-stills.mjs` after changing the film (it reads the timeline from
`src/Campaign.tsx`).

## Before and after

`before-after/<page>-before.jpg` and `<page>-after.jpg` are the same frame, laptop and position, with only the
screen changed (old destinytees.uk page, then the new one), so they line up exactly for a slider: put
`-after` underneath and reveal it by clipping `-before` from the left. The "Before" pill sits bottom right and
"After" bottom left, so each label stays on its own side of the handle. Pages: home, visit, whatson, give,
alpha and shop (old shop.destinytees.uk). Give and Alpha are not in the current cut of the film.
The raw screenshots without the laptop are in `public/site/old-*.jpg` and `new-*.jpg`.

## Frames

The iPhone and MacBook frames are Apple's product bezels. Apple's licence allows showing them in images of
mock-ups like these, but not redistributing the bezel files themselves, which is why `public/bezels/` is
gitignored. Destiny One appears for iOS only.
